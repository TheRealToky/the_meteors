## 4. Shaders, Audio & Procedural Systems
- **Procedural Terraced Hills Shader**: Custom procedural generation with smooth agricultural terrace S-curves and Rwandan highland watercolor palette.
- **Atmospheric Sky & Particle System**: Custom multi-color zenith-to-horizon GLSL shader dome, Kenney equirectangular backdrop integration, and floating ethereal spore particles inspired by *Sky: Children of the Light*.
- **Traditional Rwandan Architectural Pavilions**: Procedural *Inzu y'Umwami* thatched conical pavilions, clay walls, and stone cairn waymarkers with soft pulsing lanterns.
- **Stylized Traveler Rig**: Integrated KayKit rigged character with custom smooth materials, animated cape flutter, glowing eyes, and life-stage scaling.
- **5-Strip Crowned Walkway**: Procedurally draped organic road ribbon with soft meadow edge blending.
- **Infographic Visualizations**: Interactive D3.js demographic visualizations representing Rwanda CRVS 2025 statistical data.
